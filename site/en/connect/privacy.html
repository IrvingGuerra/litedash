LiteDash Connect privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Applies to LiteDash Connect for Android, the phone app that goes with LiteDash. The car screen app has its own LiteDash policy.

In short

LiteDash Connect stores the minimum and never sells or shares your data. It has no ads or analytics of its own. To use it you sign in with your account (Google or email); we only store what's explained below. Everything else is talking to the car screen you linked, over your local network, through an encrypted channel. Apart from your account, the reports you choose to send and the car catalog, the only thing that goes to the internet is anonymous technical data that Google's QR code reader (ML Kit) sends to Google about how it works; see below too.

Your account

You sign in with Google or with email and password. We store your name, your email, how you signed in (Google or email), the app version, its language and the sign-up and last-use dates; if you sign in with Google, also your profile photo. Your password is stored encrypted by Firebase: we never see it. The screen you link is also stored with your account, with no data about you: its identifier, its model, its LiteDash version and the catalog car it shows.

It's used to register your account and, later on, to back up your LiteDash. It's stored in Google's Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). It's not shared, not sold and not used for ads. You can delete it anytime from the app (Your account → Privacy and data → Delete my account) or at Delete your account.

Camera

The camera is used only to read the QR code shown by your car's screen (Settings → Phone). The permission is requested only when you tap "Link my screen". The image is read on your phone at that moment: no photos are taken, no images are saved and nothing is sent anywhere.

The link with your screen

When you link, the app saves on your phone: the screen's identifier, the link key, your car's name and the screen model (and the date you linked). With LiteDash 1.4.147 or newer, the screen also sends your car as it looks there (model, color, finish and a picture); it is saved only on your phone to show it on the home screen even when you're not connected. The key is encrypted with the Android keystore. The key never leaves your phone; of the screen, your account only stores what Your account says.

The connection

Your phone and the linked screen talk directly over the local network: your phone's hotspot or the same Wi‑Fi. To find each other, the phone announces itself on that local network. Everything they exchange is encrypted with the link key, and the phone only accepts the screen you linked. Nothing goes through the internet or anyone's servers. That's why the app asks for network and Wi‑Fi state permissions.

Your music on the screen

If you tap "Allow music", Android gives the app "notification access". With it, LiteDash Connect reads only what is playing in your music apps (title, artist, album, app name, cover art, length and whether it is paused) to show it on the linked screen, and passes the buttons you tap on the screen (play, pause, next, previous and seek) to that app. It does not read the text of your notifications or your messages, and it keeps nothing. That data only travels, encrypted, to your screen over your local network, and only while they are connected: it never reaches the internet or our servers. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Your cars

You can link several screens (My cars); for each one your phone keeps what "The link with your screen" says. With a screen connected you can change its car, color, finish and name from the app: the change travels encrypted over your local network and the screen applies it. To show you the catalog, the app downloads the list of cars and their pictures from GitHub (where LiteDash publishes its cars); if your screen has no internet, the app also downloads the car you picked and hands it over the local network. GitHub sees your phone's internet address, like any page you visit; we send it nothing of yours.

Your car's status

If your screen has it turned on, it sends a snapshot of how your car is doing: the current trip (kilometers and minutes), how many trouble codes there are, battery voltage, engine temperature and tire pressures. It is kept only on your phone (the latest one per screen) so you can see it while not connected. It is not uploaded to the internet.

Reports

Only if you tap "Send a report", the topic and note you write go to our Firebase (Firestore) database, plus technical diagnostics to find the problem: app and Android version, phone model, its networks (type, local addresses and whether there is a hotspot or VPN, without Wi‑Fi names), the state of the link and of the linked screens (identifier, LiteDash version, when they were seen) and the latest connection attempts; if a screen is connected, its diagnostics too. The report carries no name, email or location, and we only read it to fix problems.

Link notification

While a screen is linked, the app keeps the link alive with a discreet, persistent notification ("Link with your car"), also after restarting the phone. It's how Android lets the connection continue with the app closed. With no link, the app doesn't run in the background.

How to delete everything

Tap Unlink in the app: the saved link is deleted and the screen is notified. You can also uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings; the link isn't kept anywhere else. Your account is deleted separately, from the app or at Delete your account: it's deleted right away and only an anonymous record remains (dates and whether you used Google or email).

Third parties

The QR code reader is Google ML Kit and reads the code on your phone: camera images never leave it. ML Kit does send Google, encrypted, technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics of that reader: the phone's make, model and Android version, the app's name and version, a per-installation identifier that can't tell who you are, and metrics such as how long it took or which error occurred. Google doesn't share it with third parties (ML Kit data disclosure). Your account lives in Google's Firebase, as explained above. The reports you send go to Firestore, as explained above, and the car catalog is downloaded from GitHub. Other than that, LiteDash Connect includes no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK and sends no data to any server of ours. If you install it from Google Play, Google may collect store data under its own policy. This page is hosted on Firebase Hosting, which may log technical data about the visit.

Children

LiteDash Connect is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If a new version uses any data differently, this policy is updated before it ships.
