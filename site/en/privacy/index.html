Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Applies to LiteDash for Android (Google Play and GitHub).

In short

LiteDash does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no ads, no accounts, and no permission to use the internet.

What the app uses on your device

  • The list of installed apps, only to show them in the drawer and open them when you tap. That list never leaves your device.
  • The system time and language, to show the clock and date.
  • Your settings and home layout (pieces, colors, dock apps). They are stored only on your device.
  • Music volume, to show and change it from the dock.

Music (optional)

If you add the Music piece and tap "Turn on", Android will ask you to give LiteDash notification access. That is how Android lets an app see what is playing in other apps. LiteDash only reads the song, artist, cover art and playback controls; it does not read the content of your notifications, store them or send them anywhere. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Optional modules

Future features (for example an OBD scanner, tire sensors, GPS or weather) will be off by default. Each one will ask for its permission only when you turn it on, explain why, and this policy will be updated before it ships.

Third parties

LiteDash includes no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you install it from Google Play, Google may collect store data under its own policy. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical visit data.

Children

LiteDash is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
