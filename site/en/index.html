Your car is the background.

Your car sits on the floor with its shadow and a soft reflection running under the cards. Like in luxury cars, but on your screen.

  • Ten models in the catalog (cars and a motorcycle), with their badges. Download one only if you want it.
  • Any color: hue, saturation and brightness, live.
  • Spin it 360° with your finger; it stays where you leave it. One tap shows it big.
  • Prefer something else? Your logo or an image of yours instead of the car, or your car on the road with your speed and trip distance.
  • Don't want to see a car? Speedometer-only templates, or your photo as the background with cards on top, BYD style.

Paint it like yours.

Six quick colors and a free picker for any shade. The app doesn't ship with cars: they download from the catalog (about 2 MB each) and go away with one tap whenever you want.

  • Preview every car before downloading it.
  • Spin quality Low or High: more smoothness downloads only the missing frames.
  • You can always go back to the drawn sedan, which weighs nothing.

The Workshop: your OBD scanner.

Plug an ELM327 adapter into the OBD port (under the steering wheel) and LiteDash talks to the engine. Over Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi or a USB cable, with no extra app.

  • Trouble codes explained in plain words, and clearing them with a double confirmation.
  • Would you pass inspection? The check engine light and the readiness monitors they check.
  • Gauges on your home screen: tachometer, coolant and oil temperature, fuel, battery, fuel economy, boost, throttle and fuel rate.
  • Alerts if the engine overheats or the battery isn't charging, and a speedometer from OBD or GPS.

Modular: everything starts off.

Freshly installed, LiteDash is the bare minimum. Each feature turns on when you want it, and what's off costs nothing: no memory, no battery, no permissions. All inside the same app, nothing else to install.
