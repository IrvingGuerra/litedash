Your car is the background.

Your car stands on the floor, with its shadow and a subtle reflection running under the cards. Like in luxury cars, but on your screen.

  • Pick your model from the catalog and download it only if you want it.
  • Any color: hue, saturation and brightness, live.
  • Spin it 360° with your finger. You choose how smooth: Light, Medium or Maximum.
  • Light and dark mode.

Paint it like yours.

Six quick colors and a free picker for any shade. The app doesn't carry the cars: they download from the catalog (from 1.2 MB) and you remove them with one tap whenever you like.

  • A preview of every car before you download it.
  • More smoothness only downloads the missing frames, nothing twice.
  • You can always go back to the drawn sedan, which weighs nothing.

Modular: everything starts off.

Freshly installed, LiteDash is the bare minimum. Each feature turns on when you want it, and what's off costs nothing: no memory, no battery, no permissions. All inside the same app, nothing else to install.
